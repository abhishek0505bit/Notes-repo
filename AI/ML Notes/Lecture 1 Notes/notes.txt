How machine learning works: 
    basically we take the historical data, and label it(provide the output to that data), and then we train the machine learning model on that data. 
    and then we can use the model to predict the output for the new data.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Supervised learning: 
    It has two types: 
        - Regression: focuses on the numerical output/ values. 
            - Linear Regression:
            - Ridge
            - Lasso
        - Classification: focuses on the categorical output/ values. (yes/ no kind of op )
            for eg. whether a person is diabetic or not (yes/ no)
                - Trees
                - SVM 
                - KNN

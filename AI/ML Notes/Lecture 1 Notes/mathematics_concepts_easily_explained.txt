standard deviation: 
    Standard deviation tells us how spread out the data is from its average (mean).
    In simple words: 
        - Low standard deviation → values are close to the mean
        - High standard deviation → values are far from the mean
    It tells us: 
        On average, how far are the data points from the center?
        Standard deviation measures how spread out data is from the mean, and ML uses it to normalize features so no feature dominates just because of scale.

    OPTIONAL: [Step by step explanation of standard deviation]
        Real-life intuition

            1️⃣ Imagine two classes:

                Class A marks:
                48, 49, 50, 51, 52

                Class B marks:
                10, 30, 50, 70, 90


                Both have the same average (mean = 50)
                But:

                    Class A students are very similar → low spread

                    Class B students are very different → high spread

                    👉 Standard deviation captures this spread

            2️⃣ How do we calculate standard deviation? (step-by-step)

                Let’s say we have this data:

                X = [2, 4, 6, 8]

                🔹 Step 1: Calculate the mean
                    mean = (2 + 4 + 6 + 8) / 4 = 5
      
                🔹 Step 2: Find deviation from the mean

                    Subtract the mean from each value:

                    Value	Deviation (x − mean)
                    2	        −3
                    4	        −1
                    6	        +1
                    8	        +3

                🔹 Step 3: Square the deviations

                    (so negatives don’t cancel positives)

                    Deviation	Squared
                    −3	            9
                    −1	            1
                    +1	            1
                    +3	            9

                🔹 Step 4: Take the average of squared deviations

                    (This is called variance)
                    variance= (9+1+1+9)/4 = 5

                🔹 Step 5: Take the square root
                    standard deviation = square root of 5 ≈ 2.24            
                    ✅ That’s it — you just computed standard deviation.

            3️⃣ What does this number (2.24) mean?

                It means:

                On average, the values are about 2.24 units away from the mean (5).

                So most values lie roughly between:

                5 − 2.24  and  5 + 2.24
                ≈ 2.76 to 7.24


                Which matches our data [2, 4, 6, 8].

            4️⃣ Why standard deviation is used in ML (VERY IMPORTANT)

                Remember this formula from scaling:
                    scaled value = (x−mean) / standard deviation
                
                    ​

                What this does:

                    Subtract mean → centers data at 0

                    Divide by std → normalizes spread

                After scaling:

                    Mean becomes 0

                    Std deviation becomes 1

                    This makes different features comparable.
